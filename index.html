What you'll learn

Three tracks, taught in parallel by people running these systems in production. Pick one to start, or move between them as your interests shift.

DevOps Foundations

Build and break real CI/CD pipelines: containers, infrastructure as code, and rollbacks that actually work under pressure.

  • Git, GitHub Actions, and Jenkins
  • Docker and Kubernetes in production
  • Zero-downtime deploys with Argo CD

AI Engineering

Move past prompt tinkering into systems: evals, retrieval, and the plumbing that keeps models reliable in production.

  • Automations in n8n and Airflow
  • RAG, agents, and tool calling
  • Evals with Ragas and Promptfoo

Cloud & Infrastructure

Design systems that hold up: observability, cost control, and incident response for the infrastructure underneath it all.

  • Terraform and Ansible on real infra
  • Prometheus, Grafana, and the ELK stack
  • Secrets with Vault and SOPS

How a cohort runs

Four steps, repeated every week, from your first session to your first production deploy.

01

Join a track

Pick DevOps, AI Engineering, or Cloud & Infra, and get matched into a small working group.

02

Learn live

Attend a weekly workshop taught by a practitioner, with the session recorded for later.

03

Build with a partner

Apply what you learned to a real project, paired with someone at a similar stage.

04

Ship & get reviewed

Deploy your work and get it reviewed by a mentor before you move to the next module.

Questions people ask before joining

Do I need DevOps or AI experience already? +

No. Each track starts from working fundamentals and moves at the pace of your cohort. You'll need basic comfort with the command line and one programming language.

How much time does a cohort take each week? +

Plan for one 90-minute workshop and 3–4 hours of paired build time. Workshops are recorded, so a missed week won't put you behind.

Is OpsMind free to join? +

Yes. Workshops and community access are free. Some deep-dive cohorts with live mentor review are limited in size to keep feedback fast.
